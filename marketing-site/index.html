Questions we hear a lot

New to hiring a cleaner? Here's what most people want to know before booking.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

Not at all. Plenty of clients hand off a key, a lockbox code, or a garage code and go about their day — we're comfortable working with whatever access setup feels right for you.

What if I'm not happy with something?

Tell us and we'll make it right — we stand behind every job with a 100% satisfaction guarantee.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, we bring everything needed for the clean. If you'd rather we use natural products, just say so when you book — no extra charge.

How do I get an exact price?

Because every home is different, we do a quick walkthrough before quoting — that way the price you're given is the price you pay, no surprises once we're on site.

What if I need to reschedule?

Life happens — just reach out as soon as you know and we'll find a new time that works.

Ready for a cleaner space?

Send over a few details and we'll follow up to schedule a quick home visit — a walkthrough lets us give you an exact, detailed quote. Or just call — we're always happy to talk it through.
